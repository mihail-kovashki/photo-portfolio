## Mapping

Trip "Hokkaido · Autumn 2025", a side trip from the Seoul stay, 28 Sep to 1 Oct, based
in Sapporo.

- **28 Sep:** Sapporo, from Odori and the clock tower up to the JR Tower observatory at
  blue hour, then the city at night.
- **29 Sep:** Shakotan in the morning (Cape Kamui, then the Shimamui coast), Otaru in the
  afternoon, Shiroi Koibito Park, and Odori at dusk.
- **30 Sep:** the Blue Pond and Shikisai-no-oka in Biei, then Farm Tomita in Furano.
- **1 Oct:** Jozankei gorge, the Hill of the Buddha, Hokkaido Jingu, Shiroi Koibito Park
  again, and the beer museum.

My calls:
- **Jozankei and the Hill of the Buddha stay under Sapporo** as chapters. They're day
  trips, but within Sapporo.
- **Biei and Furano are one place.** They're neighbouring towns, seen on one day.
- **Cape Kamui names the Shakotan set,** including the Shimamui frames, because it's the
  name I know it by.

## The selection (84)

### Sapporo (38, five chapters, in site order: the city, then the Buddha, Jingu, Jozankei, Shiroi Koibito)

  more specific places. The set's ratings also ran slightly high (3184 especially; see
