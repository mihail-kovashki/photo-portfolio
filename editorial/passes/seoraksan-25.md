## Mapping

Trip "Sokcho · Autumn 2025", a side trip from the Seoul stay. The place is Seoraksan, not
Sokcho: the national park is the most specific name with enough photos. Almost all one
long day, 23 Oct, 08:45 to 16:25: the cable car to Gwongeumseong, the Buddha and the
valley, Ulsanbawi, the maples on the way down, Sinheungsa, Cheonbuldong into the cloud,
then Biryong and Towangseong falls. Plus 7 coast frames from 21–22 Oct.
