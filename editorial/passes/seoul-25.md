## Mapping

The three-month Seoul stay is its own trip, "Seoul · Autumn 2025"; trips out from it
(Sokcho, Hokkaido, Beijing, Japan) are separate trips. This was the first place through
the editorial pass, and it set most of the rules in [`../README.md`](../README.md).

## How it went

- Claude's first chapter sheets had 7 chapters and 36 photos. Too many chapters: the
  palaces belong together, the Wall joins Gwanghwamun (it's outside the palace, part of
  the square), and the flower field and the Anguk night merge into one evening walk.
  Result: 5 chapters, 37 photos.
- Claude rated the two uncut days (the Han on 14 Sep, Achasan on 21 Sep) close to my own
  picks. 5★: 2212, 2347 (Namsan silhouette) and 2369 (Lotte Tower in warm light), which
  led to measuring 5★ against the portfolio rather than the session.
- Corrections: 2367 is the Gangnam towers, not Yeouido, and only a 3; Claude missed 2307;
  towers count as `new-architecture`. I reminded Claude it was judging finished edits,
  not RAWs.
- The chapters were first in date order; Achasan, the strongest, came last. That led to
  "open with the strongest chapter".
