## Proposed 5★

**4470**: the Forbidden City from Jingshan at dusk. The Shenwu Gate is lit gold on the axis,
and behind it hundreds of roofs darken layer by layer to the city's lights under a violet-to-peach
sky. The symmetry is exact, the gate is the one bright point, and the roofs give it depth all
the way to the horizon. I checked it against the site's twelve on one sheet, and it sits beside
Prague 7253 (the Castle over the bridge at blue hour) as an equal. Asked what could have been
done better from there, the answer is very little. Against it: the top third is a plain gradient,
and it's the view every visitor to Jingshan takes. Under the rules that costs nothing, but it's
your call. That would make two 5★ for the trip, with 4002.

## What I took from it

The held pile mattered less this time: one frame changed its scene, against twenty last time.
It worked because the shortlist was generous, not because holding is safe. Focus mattered more:
three frames looked right at 1400px and failed at 100%, and two of them were flowers or near
foregrounds, the cases `taste.md` already names. Where there's a near subject in front of the
main one, the crop has to go on the near subject. I gave 4361's first crop to the boat and nearly
missed it. And six frames went from 4 to 3 in a second pass over the 4s, because similar frames
(four lit pavilions, two roof-from-below) had each been judged alone. Seeing a scene's 4s together
before writing the chapters would have caught that sooner.
