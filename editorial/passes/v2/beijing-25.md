## What the close look found

  ratings file has your star beside mine as `cull`. Where we differ, it's in the critique.
- **Focus.** Clean all day; haze softens the distance, which isn't a miss. One real miss:
  **3936** (the sunlit parapet in front of the ridge). The focus landed on the far slope, and
  the near parapet, which the frame is built on, is soft. Down to 3 and parked; it holds at
  display size. The duck in 3893 is motion-soft, which is fine for a pattern frame.
- **No dust** in the skies checked (3833, 3992, 4057, 3899).
- **Grey Beihai, judged by what the light does.** It gave the day's quietest frames (3899
  the single duck, 3900 the pink robe under the willow, 3851 the glazed roofs). The ones that
  worked kept the sky small or made the haze the subject. The wide reflections (3842, 3871)
  stay 3s because half of each frame is blank sky.

  Each pair now has a pick: 3941 over 3921 and 3943; 4002 over 4000 and 4009; 3999 is
  4002's portrait take. 3801 and 3812 turned out to be **the same ceiling**, not two

## What I took from it

The ratings found less than the reading of the files did. Your cull stars were sitting in the
exports, and session A judged the sheet without them. That cost two of your 4★s and ten of
your 3★s, four of which earn a place now. The first look should read the files' existing
ratings as evidence, the way it reads Instagram. Mutianyu showed the other side of
"other orientation is never a twin": keeping both takes is right for the ratings, but on the
page they read as a repeat, so the page carries one and parks the other.
