## Proposed chapters

**Český Krumlov** (32, was 20 with no chapters). Four chapters. The days' order would put the
viewpoint first and the river last anyway, but the chapters are by place, not by hour: the
views were shot at three times of day and the castle twice. A city opens with the city, and
the views are also the strongest chapter.

1. **Over the river bend** (7): 7542 · 7605 · 7498 · 7533 · 7523 · 7528 · 7603.
   The whole town from the castle garden, then all three towers in one, the same town through
   the bridge's stone window, St Jošt's dome from the tower, straight down on the roofs and on
   Latrán's loop of river, and the river under the footbridge with the tower over it to close.
   Average 4.0.
2. **The old town** (9): 7469 · 7473 · 7490 · 7476 · 7564 · 7586 · 7587 · 7567 · 7562.
   St Jošt in front of the castle to say where we are, two lanes to the tower (Masná with the
   boulders, the yellow lane), the stepped lane with the lantern, the square, the narrowest
   lane in sun, St Vitus with the laundry, the white arch stairs, and St Vitus inside to close.
   7564 is the weakest; it's here because the two tower lanes say the castle is always at the
   end of the street. Average 3.7.
3. **The castle** (9): 7526 · 7554 · 7609 · 7537 · 7540 · 7519 · 7516 · 7514 · 7511.
   The whole castle on its rock from the tower, the cloak bridge from below, the painted
   courtyard, the coffered ceiling, the golden carriage, the rock-cut passage, then the
   theatre: a box, the machinery under the stage, and the stage itself to close. Average 3.7.
4. **On the Vltava** (7): 7595 · 7578 · 7576 · 7589 · 7593 · 7598 · 7602.
   The castle mirrored in still water, the wall and the unicorn, the terrace to St Jošt, the
   canoe landing, the roses and the kayak, the weir sweeping to the tower, and the yellow raft
   under the castle to close. Average 3.7.

The other way to read it: split the castle into outside and the theatre with the rooms, but
the outside is three frames. Or put the river second, after the views, so the two strongest
chapters lead; I'd keep the town second, so the page goes from above to the streets to the
castle to the water.

## What I took from it

Shooting the same view at three times of day only pays if the light changes, and here it
mostly didn't: the evening views from the castle (7604, 7611) lost to the 13:58 frame, when
the clouds broke. The castle's interiors carry it the way St Barbara's did in Kutná Hora,
and the river is where people make the place feel like summer.

## After my review (2026-10-08)

Applied: Český Krumlov 20 → 27, four chapters. I'm being more selective: not much more, but
more.

- **Toss-ups:** 7605 over 7549; 7564 out (one tower lane too many); 7465 stays parked.
- **The old town** (8): 7469 · 7473 · 7490 · 7584 · 7586 · 7587 · 7567 · 7562. 7476 is out,
  more documentary than a good shot, and 7584 does the same square much better. It had been
  rated 2★ from the sheet for its cloud shadow and never looked at; at 1000px the late light
  on the gables and the textured sky carry it, and the shadow leads in. Up to 3★.
- **The castle was overrated** (5): 7526 · 7579 · 7609 · 7537 · 7511. Out: 7516 (not
  high-ISO mush so much as a weak photo, down to 2★), 7514 (an interesting thing to shoot,
  meh as a photo, 3★), 7519 (3★) and 7540 (2★), neither interesting nor well shot. 7579, the
  cloak bridge in its street with the people resting, is in over 7554 from below; I like both
  without loving either, so both are 3★.
- **7578** is a 3★, the unicorn being fun doesn't make it more. It keeps its place.

What I took from it: an interesting subject (a Baroque stage's machinery, a golden carriage)
talked up a photo that wasn't good; the rating has to be of the frame, not of the thing in it.
And twice now a frame marked down on the sheet for one flaw (7110, 7584) turned out the better
photo once it was seen at size.
