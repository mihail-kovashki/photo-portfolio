## What I took from it

The cost of the views in this album wasn't the images but the context: the first four 1400px views
dropped out before their notes were written, and I had to look at them again side by side. Writing
each batch's ratings before opening the next would have saved that. The rest held: one view shot
thirteen times collapsed cleanly once the dusk frames were in, and twins at 1000px settled every run
without a re-look.
