## What the closer look found

- **Shot through glass.** 4871 and 4964 were taken from the cable car, and the cabin's
  reflections sit across the sky. Invisible on a contact sheet; obvious at full size. Both
  are on the site in "Yeosu by dusk".
- **Sensor dust.** 5034 has a dark spot in the sky that shows at display size, so it's a
  defect on the live page. 5017 has a smaller one, also visible. 5030 has specks.
- **The posted cover is soft.** 6390, the cover of the Seoul post, is soft on the couple at
  100%: a near miss or slight shake. At the site's 2048px it's fine; printed it wouldn't be.
- **A posted frame rated 1★.** The second Seoul slide is 6417-2, the 4:5 edit. The first
  pass called every `-2` file a duplicate. A second edit can be the preferred one.
- **Frames that grew.** At full size 4987 (azaleas) and 5002 (hydrangeas) are crisp and
  layered, not filler; 6392 (the kayak in the sun's path) and 6449 (the 63 Building's gold
  glass) are stronger than their thumbnails.

## Presentation

- Yeosu's chapters are now three, five and three photos. Under the rules that argues for
  two chapters, or one set like Seoul 24. I'd keep the three names, because they're yours
  and leave room for more Yeosu, but it's a real tension with "fewer, fuller chapters".
- Seoul 24 has no chapters and works as an evening in order. The cut makes it read faster.
- The page titles still say "Seoul 24" and "Yeosu 24"; that's the deferred naming work.

## Comparison with the first pass

The first pass was right about the best frames: 4953, 5027, 6343, 6390, 6430 and 6442 all
kept their 4★. It was wrong at the margins in both directions: it kept five frames with
defects you can see on the site (glass, dust), and it underrated four that only show
their quality at full size. It also missed the Instagram evidence entirely.

## After my review (2026-10-01)

Applied to the site:

- **Out:** 4871 (the glass blur is the photo), 6358 (one tower too many), 6387 (I like it,
  partly for the landscape format, but 6390 says it better).
- **Kept against the proposal:** 4890, whose softness on the YEOSU sign is creative rather
  than a miss; 5014, where the leaves are a blemish but the frame has enough mood; 5017 and
  5034, whose dust goes on the edit queue instead (the chapter stays full); 6381, which is
  busy, but the sun isn't lost in haze. It plays beautifully with the clouds, the light is
  great, and the busy park has small human moments. Claude misread that light.
- **Ratings in the files:** 4987 and 5002 up to 3★, 6392 and 6449 up to 4★.

Result: Seoul 24 has 11 photos, Yeosu 15 (5 · 5 · 5).

What this changed in the process: the first look moved to 600px sheets (the size test in
the editorial README), flaws now come in three kinds (disqualifying, a blemish, fixable)
and only the first removes a frame, and the order rule weighs story against the best
photo instead of always leading with the strongest. Under that rule both Korea pages keep
their order: each is a sequence whose opener already holds the first screen.
