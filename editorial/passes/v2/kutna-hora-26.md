One day, 4 Jul 2026, a day trip from Prague, the first place of "Czechia · Summer 2026" to
go through the pass. The source is `Czechia 26/selects/Kutna Hora`: the 81 frames that
survived the cull, plus 7034 and 7002, copied back from `raw/` because they were posted (each
had lost to a near-twin in the cull). These are camera JPEGs, not Lightroom exports: the
recipe's grain and colour are the edit.

- **St Barbara's** outside (09:38–09:51, 10:40–10:49) and inside (09:58–10:31), with the
  gallery and a vaulted upper room.

  Fountain, the Stone House, the red alley, the painted house.

## Proposed chapters

**Kutná Hora** (33, was 28 with no chapters). Five chapters, in the order of the day: the
story wins, and the opener is the town's landmark as you approach it.

1. **St Barbara's** (7): 6977 · 7034 · 6987 · 6985 · 6995 · 7031 · 7026.

   the west front over the flowerbed, the sun over the apse, the buttresses in B&W, the
   pinnacles, along the side wall, and the whole silhouette from the terrace with a couple
   walking away to close. Average 3.4.
2. **Inside St Barbara's** (8): 7009 · 7016 · 7005 · 7014 · 7015 · 7017 · 7018 · 7019.
   The nave to the altar to say where we are, the miner and the tabernacle, the window
   throwing its colour, the frescoed side chapel, the star vault, the apse vault, the organ,
   and the quiet vaulted room upstairs to close. Average 4.0, the strongest chapter.
3. **The old town** (7): 7045 · 7051 · 7054 · 7062 · 7079 · 6973 · 7068.
   From the vineyard over St James down into the town: the pink-and-yellow lane, the Stone
   Fountain, the Stone House, the painted house, then two lanes, the quiet one and the red
   alley, which closes. 6973 is from 09:35 and moved here; it's a lane, not the morning.
   Average 3.3.
4. **The Italian Court** (5): 7081 · 7085 · 7101 · 7090 · 7076.
   The garden and the tower, the oriel and the stair, a heraldic window, the red bench under
   the hydrangeas, and St Barbara through the gate to close: the court looks back to where
   the day began. Five, so at the floor. Average 3.4.
5. **Sedlec** (6): 7112 · 7116 · 7124 · 7118 · 7122 · 7126.
   The west front, the nave from the choir, the spiral stair up, the arches from the gallery,
   the nave from the gallery, and straight up the vault to close. Average 3.7.

The other way to read the page: fold the Italian Court into the old town (one continuous
walk), which gives four chapters but a twelve-photo middle. I'd keep five.

## What I took from it

The cull has to look at posted frames side by side with their twins rather than trusting
time order: it dropped 7034, which was the better frame. And on a bright overcast day the
interiors carry the place; outside, a frame that keeps the sky small (6995, 7068, 7085)
beats the wide view every time.

What I took from it: rating from a sheet let the sky decide 7110; a frame dropped there for
one flaw never gets the look that would show what it has. And a documentary frame of a
landmark (7054) is a 2, however famous the landmark.
