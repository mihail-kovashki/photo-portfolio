Five days in Prague, 2–8 July 2026 (4 and 7 Jul were Kutná Hora and Český Krumlov). It is
the last of the three places in "Czechia · Summer 2026". The source is
`Czechia 26/selects/Prague`: 436 camera JPEGs (433 cull survivors, plus 6876, 6864 and
6668, which had been posted and were copied back). The recipe is the edit.

- **2 Jul**: Vinohrady and Žižkov. St Ludmila, the villas, Jiřího z Poděbrad, the TV
  tower, the Jewish cemetery. Then evening gold round Riegrovy sady, and the main station at
  dusk.
- **3 Jul**: the Castle (tower, St Vitus, Vladislav Hall, Golden Lane), Nový Svět, Malá
  Strana and Wallenstein Garden. In the evening: Loreta, Kampa, Charles Bridge at sunset and
  blue hour, Old Town Square in the last sun, trams at night. Both Instagram posts are from
  this day.
- **5 Jul**: overcast. The Old Town Hall tower, Josefov, the embankment at dusk, the castle
  lights coming on. From about 22:00, the olive Nostalgic Neg experiment.
- **6 Jul**: overcast. Karlín, Vrtba Garden, Čertovka, the narrowest lane, Letná.
- **8 Jul**: Vyšehrad, then the Strahov library.

gardens chapter you asked for. They're by district, not by day. The page goes from the
Castle down through Malá Strana and its gardens, across the river, into the Old Town, and
out east. A city opens with the city: 6688, from the cathedral tower, opens the page.

## What I took from it

Five days of Prague at 1000px showed the same thing as Kutná Hora: on the grey days (5 and
6 Jul) the frames that work keep the sky small and find a pattern or a frame. The parterre,
Čertovka under the arch, the stacked bridges. The 3 Jul evening does the rest, because the
light was there. The posts chose well for the Castle and less well for the evening: half of
that post is facades in nice light, which is a nice-light photo, not a good one.
