A second run of Prague from scratch, without opening the first run's log, ratings, first look
or tags, the re-read or the re-cull file, to test `taste.md`'s new critique rules ("How to
critique", "What costs a frame"). The source is `Czechia 26/selects/Prague`: 449 camera JPEGs
from five days, 2–8 Jul 2026.

- **2 Jul:** Vinohrady and Žižkov in hard sun, then Riegrovy sady at sunset.
- **3 Jul:** Hradčany and the castle, Malá Strana and Kampa, the Old Town at golden hour, the
  bridge at blue hour and Malá Strana at night.
- **5 Jul:** the Old Town Hall tower under grey cloud, Josefov and the river, the castle at
  blue hour, the Old Town at night.
- **6 Jul, grey:** Karlín, Vrtba Garden, the Čertovka, the narrowest lane, Letná.
- **8 Jul:** Vyšehrad, Charles Bridge at noon, the Strahov library.

Three sittings: session A (sheets for all 449: 118 at 2★, 125 shortlisted, 206 held, 58
near-twin runs, 27 settled side by side at 1000px); session B, sitting 1 (2–3 Jul: 80
shortlisted plus 11 pulled); sitting 2 (5–8 Jul: 45 shortlisted plus 7 pulled from a 600px
sheet of the held frames). 143 frames rated at 1400px with 100% crops. Nothing on the site
has changed; this is a proposal.

**Not fully blind.** `taste.md` names nine Prague frames with verdicts (6853, 6509, 6897,
7400, 7253, 7353, 7456, 6760/6764), the cull notes name the re-cull's recoveries, a slip in
session A showed the re-read's picks (6762, 6740, 6958, 6838, 7457), and the vault note's edit
queue names 7226's dust spot. These are scored separately in the comparison.

  20, seven reach the selection; the castle post's cover, 6659, is a 3 (a man's phone held up

  walker in 7400 is blurred. 7274 is selected knowingly; at display size it holds.

## Proposed chapters

**Prague** (51), six chapters, past the soft cap of five as the README allows for a big city
with a garden chapter. A city opens with the city, and the river is where Prague is all at
once: the bridges, the castle, the Old Town's towers.

1. **On the Vltava** (7): 7436 · 7428 · 6925 · 6944 · 7226 · 7258 · 7253.
   The bridges stacked down the river from Letná, the people on the parapet looking at them,
   the castle across the water as the light goes, the statue on the bridge, deep twilight
   with one pedalo, the red embankment house under storm blue, and the castle over the lit
   arches to close. One evening's light running down, though it's three evenings. Average 3.9.
2. **The castle and Hradčany** (9): 6674 · 6638 · 6706 · 6682 · 6597 · 6733 · 6568 · 6811 · 7669.
   St Vitus's apse as rhythm, the nave to the rose window, straight up into Vladislav Hall,
   straight down onto the courtyard, Golden Lane as a colour field, the Castle Steps, the lane
   of light at Nový Svět, Loreta in the last sun, and Strahov's Theological Hall to close.
   Average 4.0, the strongest chapter.
3. **Malá Strana and Kampa** (11): 6762 · 7393 · 6836 · 6835 · 6832 · 6849 · 6851 · 6853 · 6852 · 6958 · 6964.
   The lane under the arch, the Čertovka through the bridge, two streets to their towers, St
   Nicholas in gold, then Kampa's houses in the last sun, the orange wall, the gable, the
   statue in the slot, and into the night: the arcade's lamps, the tram on its rails. Day
   into night in one walk. Average 3.7.
4. **Vrtba Garden** (5): 7378 · 7360 · 7361 · 7368 · 7366.
   The garden under the castle, the whole design from the stairs, the parterre from straight
   above, the terrace's diagonal to the city, and Malá Strana's roofs to close. Grey all
   through, and it suits it. Average 3.4.
5. **The Old Town** (10): 7133 · 7134 · 7159 · 6874 · 6885 · 6888 · 6871 · 6868 · 7274 · 7282.
   The roofs from the Old Town Hall tower, the crowd at the clock from above, the lift shaft
   inside it; then the tower and Týn in the last copper light, the Storch house, St Giles
   down a dark street, Charles IV on Knights of the Cross Square, and the night lane and a
   terrace to close. Average 3.6.
6. **Vinohrady and Žižkov** (9): 6416 · 6386 · 6463 · 6442 · 6500 · 6502 · 6509 · 6528 · 6511.
   Plečnik's church, the T3 against the ochre school, the Akropolis corner, the Žižkov
   tower's babies from below; then the evening: gilded fronts through trees, the tower
   squeezed between them, the gable in the dark, and the crowd at Riegrovy sady facing the
   sunset, the walker on the path to close. Average 3.2.

The other way: the castle first on its average (4.0), the river second. I'd keep the river
first: the castle chapter is the castle and its rooms, and the river is the city. Or fold
Vrtba into Malá Strana, which would make 16, too long.

## What I took from it

The rules moved the ratings where they were meant to: frames with one lit shape and space
round it (6851, 6853, 6509, 7361) reached 4 on line and light, and landmark records in good
light stayed at 3 (6668, 6585, 7156). The grey day earned its own chapter by pattern, not by
colour. What the rules don't do on their own is pick the shortlist: 18 of the 143 frames
rated were pulled from the held pile, and four reached 4★ (6762, 6958, 7226, 7274).
