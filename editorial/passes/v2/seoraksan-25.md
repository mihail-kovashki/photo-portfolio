- **Glass again.** 3397 (Up to Gwongeumseong, on the site) has heavy cable car reflections
  across the left third and a flare patch on the cliff, visible at display size. The view
  to Sokcho and the sea is excellent, which makes it painful. 3388 is worse (1★). Your
  3391 is through glass on purpose, and that's fine.
- **Focus is not the problem here.** Everything selected is sharp, including the climbers
  in 3468 at 100%. A good day for technique.
- **Frames that grew at full size:** 3481 (the granite wall, red maples, a dead tree and the
  whole range under cloud: the best wide frame of the day), 3422 (backlit maples, the
  clearest "maple season" frame), 3520 and 3559.
- **Near-twins on the page:** 3545 / 3546 (same cliff, same cloud), 3422 / 3603 (orange

- **A misplaced frame:** 3425, the stream with Ulsanbawi in sun, is a morning frame of
  Ulsanbawi sitting in Waterfalls.
- **Distraction:** 3385's lamp-post arms at both bottom corners.

## Presentation

- **Chapter order.** Ulsanbawi first is right: it holds the 5★ and the day's best light.
  The coast second still reads oddly after the peak; it is the arrival, but sitting between
  Ulsanbawi and the cable car it interrupts the mountain. Opening the page with the coast
  (arrival → mountain) or closing with it (the night beach as an epilogue) both read
  better. My lean: close with it, so the page opens on the mountain.
- **Openers and closers.** Every chapter but Waterfalls opens well; Waterfalls opens on
  3425, which belongs elsewhere. With 3425 moved, it opens on the cascade (3532).
- **Names hold.** They say where we are. "Waterfalls" could be "Biryong and Towangseong"
  if you want it as specific as the others.

## Comparison with the first pass

The first pass's ratings hold up better here than in Seoul or Korea: focus is clean,
there's no Instagram to disagree with, and most stars survive. What it missed is the glass
on 3397 (visible only at full size), the twins that read fine as thumbnails and repetitive
on the page, and two frames that only show their quality at full size (3481, 3422).
