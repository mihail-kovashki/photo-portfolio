124 frames added to the `Seoul 25` folder after the second pass (DSCF2397–2590, 3271–3358).
The 40 frames already on the site are untouched and out of scope. Three days, all in the
same "Seoul · Autumn 2025" stay:

- **22 Sep, Suwon** (44): the Starfield library, then Hwaseong Fortress from Janganmun
  around to Hwahongmun and Yongyeon pond, the town, Haenggung palace. A day trip with
  enough frames for its own page, so it's its own place, `suwon-25`, under the Seoul trip
  (like Malmö under Copenhagen).
- **27 Sep, the Seoul International Fireworks Festival** (36), shot with a telephoto from
  Seoraeseom, away from the crowds: twenty minutes, 20:19–20:39, with the arch bridge and a
  row of silhouettes in nearly every frame.
- **19 Oct, Olympic Park** in the morning and **Naksan** at dusk (44): the kochia hill,
  autumn avenues, the lone tree and Lotte Tower; then the Wall above Heunginjimun, Ihwa,
  sunset and blue hour.

One session: 600px sheets for all 124, 66 at 1000px, 100% crops on 24 subjects plus two sky
dust checks. Nothing on the site has changed; this is a proposal.

## What the close look found

- **Instagram:** one post, "Seoul Fireworks Festival 🎆": 2589 (the cover), 2501, 2557, and
  2509 cropped to a vertical. Nothing from Suwon, Olympic Park or Naksan was posted.
- **Focus.** Everything checked is sharp where it matters. The one exception is 2566, whose
  sparks streak at 100% because the shells moved during the exposure. It reads as motion at
  display size; flagged as soft-intended. 3350's blue-hour sky is noisy at 100% and
  invisible at size. No dust in Suwon's flat skies (2419, 2434). Nothing for the edit queue.
- **Suwon was grey.** A flat white sky over almost every frame; the photos that work there
  don't depend on the sky: the gate in the arch (2401), the falls (2419), silver grass
  (2434), and the palace's roof details (2450, 2454). The wide views (2405, 2435, 2422) are
  half blank sky and pay for it.
- **Near-twin runs:** the Hwahongmun trio → 2419 (the wide, with the pavilion on the hill);
  the Yongyeon pond run of five → 2430 (the willow curtain), a toss-up with 2427; the
  cathedral → 2445; the fireworks' pink/violet moment, six frames in two edits → 2501
  (posted) plus 2508 as a landscape variation; the red version 2509 (posted) is a near-twin

  2590; the lone tree → 3313; 3340 and 3344, the same minute facing two ways → 3340, with

5. **Evening on Naksan** (7): 3329 · 3335 · 3339 · 3340 · 3350 · 3355 · 3351.
   Sunset to night along the Wall: Heunginjimun from the fountain grass, Ihwa in low light,
   the sun between the trees and the café, the sun going down over the ridges, the Wall lit
   at blue hour, the wires and the people at the corner, and Namsan at dusk to close (moved
   from before 3355, the stronger closer).

**Where they go.** That makes eight chapters on the Seoul page, over the "three to five"
rule. Seoul is a month's stay, not a trip of a few days, so I'd let it run long rather than
squeeze unrelated days together. Proposed order: Achasan · Along the Han · **Fireworks over
the Han** (river by day, then by night) · the palaces · Gwanghwamun and the Wall · Anguk ·
**Olympic Park** · **Evening on Naksan** (the page ends at night on Namsan). The other way
is to fold: fireworks into Along the Han, Naksan into the Wall chapter. Both would be
long, and each would hold two very different evenings.
