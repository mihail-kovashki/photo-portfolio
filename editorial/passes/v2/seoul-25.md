  the site a chapter name says where we are; Instagram has room to be cheeky.
- **Anguk** (9): 2216 → 2217 and 2244 → 2245 as proposed; 2232 stays and 2250 is added.
  "Not fantastic shots but they hold up to the other in the chapter."

What I took from it: I was still too quick to call a distraction disqualifying (2299's
lines, 2071's sign); a frame that does a job in the sequence (placing us, a different
vibe) can carry a blemish. When two frames are a genuine toss-up, keep both for now.
