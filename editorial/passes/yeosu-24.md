## Mapping

Part of "Korea · Spring 2024" (see [Seoul 2024](seoul-24.md)). Two days: 21 May from
18:54 (sunset from the cable car and Dolsan, the Dolsan bridge, the harbour at night) and
22 May (sunrise from the hotel, Odongdo, then Hyangiram on the cliffs). 25 frames, plus
the 24 May swimmer, which is Busan.

## The selection (16)

All three chapters are at or under "about five photos", and the names are mine, chosen to
leave room for more Yeosu later.

**Yeosu by dusk** (6, average 3.2): the strongest light, so it opens.
- **4871 ★3** – The sun over the harbour from the cable car, the red lighthouse below.
- **4890 ★3** – The "YEOSU" sign in silhouette against the sunset. Touristy, and fun.
- **4893 ★3** – The bay and hills as the sun touches them.
- **4927 ★3** – The Dolsan bridge at pink dusk, a red bus crossing.
- **4953 ★4** – The same bridge at blue hour, lit. The best frame of the trip so far.
- **4964 ★3** – The harbour at night. Closes.

**Yeosu by day** (5, average 2.6): the weakest on stars, but I like it as a set.
- **4969 ★3** – Sunrise in pink over the harbour, the hotel block in silhouette.
- **4993 ★3** – Pine branches framing the skyline across the water.
- **5000 ★3** – A minimal concrete corridor to a doorway. Graphic, empty on purpose.
- **4987 ★2** – Azaleas on the rocks. In for the set's colour.
- **5002 ★2** – Hydrangeas under the pines. Same reason.

**Hyangiram** (5, average 3.0): the cliff hermitage over the sea.
- **5017 ★3** – The main hall under the rock outcrop.
- **5027 ★4** – Painted eaves framing the sea. The chapter's reason to exist.
- **5014 ★3** – An island in the haze, from above.
- **5032 ★3** – A tree framing the bay and its boats.
- **5034 ★2** – The coastline in soft haze. Closes quietly.
